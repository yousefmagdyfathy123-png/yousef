# Task10 source content and public identity

Public product information uses Alfadata and the source's sentence explaining
Metric/U.S. Customary dimension and weight units. The engineering reference-use
notice follows the approved design. Parameter/material text keeps original
values, spelling and units. HTML is projected into plain text blocks; there is
no raw HTML, embedded asset, executable attribute or outbound-link output.
Only explicit document IDs are accepted; no path, arbitrary catalog read,
system fingerprint or licensing information is exposed.

The original About tabs also contain vendor-company descriptions, an operating
system/user/license report, acknowledgements and warranty/legal documents.
Corporate history, original-product branding, current-standards assertions and
claims of committee review are not transplanted into Alfadata's public copy.
Their original hashes remain in evidence/task10-acceptance.json.

Rights review before launch remains required separately: inspect the original
License.rtf, Acknowledgements.txt/Aknowledgements.html and Disclaimer.txt/.html
to determine any required third-party notices. This implementation neither
claims those documents establish Alfadata's rights nor replaces their named
company with Alfadata in a legal document. No public launch is part of Task10.

Task11 consumes the private source-menu and dialog models and UiEffect host
operations. Task10's coverage gate verifies route assembly and event handling;
it does not claim browser/mobile/print acceptance or complete product delivery.
