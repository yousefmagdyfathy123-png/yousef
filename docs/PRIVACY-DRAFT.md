# Draft Alfadata privacy policy

For review before publication.

Alfadata uses the account, payment and operational information needed to provide the service, maintain access and resolve account or payment problems. Alfadata does not sell customer data or use it for marketing without separate consent. Verification, password resets, deletion confirmations and closure notices are operational communications.

Alfadata stores account, access, preference, payment and operational records. Workspace storage is designed to expire seven days after the last activity. Payment and mail services receive information required to perform those services. If you open WhatsApp support, WhatsApp and the company support team can receive the information you choose to share there.

A verified account owner can request deletion after confirming their password when the published deletion policy and calendar are available. Deletion ends account and paid access. Personal account and workspace data are removed subject to published exceptions. The independent trial registry may retain a derived identifier under an approved exception to prevent a renewed trial. Financial and backup retention periods, exceptions, processors and backup expiry still require approval before this policy is published.
