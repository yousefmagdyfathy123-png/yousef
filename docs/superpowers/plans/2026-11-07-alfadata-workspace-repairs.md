## Review Focus

- A route without verified grouping must fall back to complete bounds, retaining all annotations (Task 1).
- A zoomed drawing followed by delayed asset/font resolution or device rotation must focus the current revision correctly (Task 2).
- A failed/unavailable calculator selection must not retain a previously valid guide distance (Task 3).
- A superseded search request or closing the dialog while searching must not replace later results or steal focus (Task 4).
- A route reusing a control identifier with another meaning must not receive a misleading label (Task 5).
