This inventory describes the implementation through task 18. Retention periods marked pending require approval before production deletion or closure. Keep access to PostgreSQL, backups, registry credentials and notification tooling limited to authorized operators.

## Production dependencies

Publish approved English purchase and privacy terms and the deletion calendar with its timezone and coverage. Keep policy versions and source text archived so a historical acceptance hash can be reproduced. Configure payment and mail recipients and finalize handling of open checkout links, late settlements and post-closure records before enabling closure announcements. Run the account notice worker periodically only in an approved environment. The worker makes at most four attempts per message with a fifteen-minute retry interval. SMTP has at-least-once delivery semantics when an acknowledgement is lost.

Finalize the production deletion processor, financial and trial exceptions, backup rotation and the independent restore fence before enabling production deletion. Test approved removal, retained exceptions, notification retries and restoration from an older backup. Task 18 exposes review and the internal completion boundary while the production deletion processor awaits policy approval.
